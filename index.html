Rodney Agent

Rodney Agent is a private personal assistant built for one person, Rodney, by his son Makaih. It is not a public product and does not accept sign-ups.

With Rodney's permission it reads his personal Gmail and Google Calendar so it can summarise his day, prepare drafts and keep track of follow-ups. It sends an email or changes his calendar only after he approves that specific item.
